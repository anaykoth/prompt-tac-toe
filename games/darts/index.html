  1. Move the mouse to aim. The reticle is where the dart wants to go.
  2. Hold left mouse and pull down to wind up.
  3. Flick up and release to throw. Flick speed sets power — land it in the green band.
  4. A clean, straight flick throws straight. Sideways flicks pull the dart wide.
  5. Hold Shift to walk about. You can't throw from in front of the oche.
  6. B opens the bar. Drinking pays better and aims worse.

You can also just click-and-release to use the auto power meter.
